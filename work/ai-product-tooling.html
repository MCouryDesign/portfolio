AI-Built Product Tooling

Using AI as a build partner to ship production-ready tools and client deliverables in a fraction of the usual time — without losing design craft.

RoleAI Product Design · Prototyping
Timeline2025
StackHTML/CSS/JS · Claude
FocusAI · Automation

Overview

This is an ongoing body of work exploring how far AI can push a single designer's output. Using AI as a design-and-build partner, I produce production-ready internal tools, client deliverables, and full front-ends at a speed that used to require a team.

The challenge

Deliver the full stack of a design business — research, UI, working code, brand assets, and ops — solo, fast, and repeatably, without the output feeling generated or cutting corners on craft.

Approach & process

I design the prompts, flows, and guardrails, then drive AI through a design-to-code pipeline — reviewing, art-directing, and refining every step so the result is intentional. A few of the tools and deliverables built this way:

  • Client pipeline tracker — a kanban CRM app with local persistence and MRR tracking
  • Live financial dashboard — built from real data with an interactive gap-closer
  • Branded PDF & graphic generators — pixel-consistent, on-brand collateral on demand
  • Design-to-spec pipelines — turning concepts into engineering-ready specs
  • This portfolio — a fully custom, interactive front-end

The solution

A repeatable way of working where AI handles the heavy lifting and I hold the taste, strategy, and quality bar — turning ideas into finished, branded, working artifacts the same day.

Outcome

The result is a genuinely different velocity: work that once took weeks now ships in hours, fully branded and production-ready — the edge behind everything else in this portfolio.
