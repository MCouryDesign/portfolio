AI-Built Product Tooling

Using AI as a build partner to ship production-ready design systems, product UI, and full front-ends in a fraction of the usual time — without losing design craft.

RoleAI Product Design · Prototyping
Timeline2025 – 2026
StackHTML/CSS/JS · Claude
FocusAI · Design Systems · Automation

Overview

This is an ongoing body of work exploring how far AI can push a single designer's output. Using AI as a design-and-build partner, I produce production-ready design systems, internal tools, product UI, and full front-ends at a speed that used to require a team.

The challenge

Deliver the full stack of product design — systems, UI, working code, and brand — solo, fast, and repeatably, without the output feeling generated or cutting corners on craft.

Approach & process

I design the prompts, flows, and guardrails, then drive AI through a design-to-code pipeline — reviewing, art-directing, and refining every step so the result is intentional. The work below is a real cross-section, from a full TRUCE design-token system to a self-initiated AI product concept:

  • TRUCE Design System — a complete, documented token reference
  • Web console navigation redesign — a measured before/after
  • Live insight & driving-score dashboards built from real data
  • Resource Center & design-to-spec skills — spec and QA generators
  • Lead Systems AI — a self-initiated, end-to-end product concept

The solution

A repeatable way of working where AI handles the heavy lifting and I hold the taste, strategy, and quality bar — turning ideas into finished, branded, working artifacts the same day.

01 — Design System

A full TRUCE design-token system.

A complete, documented token reference — colors, type, spacing, motion, logo variants, and platform icons — generated and refined with Claude, structured so engineering can consume it directly.

02 — Navigation Redesign

Web console navigation — before & after.

Reworking the platform's left-hand navigation from a flat text menu into a compact icon rail with clear, on-demand depth.
