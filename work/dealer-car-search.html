Case study · 2015 – 2022

Dealer Car Search

High-volume web design and development at scale — personally building 1,714 dealer websites, 308 custom logos, and 65 SEO packages as part of a five-person team.

RoleSenior Web Designer & Developer
Timeline2015 – 2022
PlatformWeb
StackHTML5 · SASS · JS

Overview

Dealer Car Search is a leading provider of websites for car dealerships. On a five-person web team, I strategized with clients, then designed and launched new sites while maintaining daily updates across a shared portfolio of 5,000+ active dealer websites.

The challenge

Deliver modern, on-brand, high-converting websites at volume — each dealer different — while the whole industry shifted to mobile-first, and keep quality consistent across thousands of live sites.

  • Ship polished sites fast without reinventing every build
  • Hold a consistent, modern standard across a huge live portfolio
  • Adapt each site to a different dealer brand

Approach & process

I built reusable templates and components, set wireframes and brand direction, and shipped responsive, production-ready builds in HTML5, SASS, CSS3, Bootstrap, JavaScript, and jQuery — a repeatable system that made volume possible without sacrificing craft.

The solution

A standardized, mobile-first experience that dealers could make their own — modern, fast, and consistent across the network.
